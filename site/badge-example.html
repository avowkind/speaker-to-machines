Embedding a badge

Add one script tag, then one <stm-badge> element per badge. The badge renders in its own shadow DOM, so your page's styles don't change it. Besides its own code it fetches nothing but the profile you point it at.

From attributes

<script type="module" src="https://YOUR-HOST/badge.js"></script>
<stm-badge code="INST" level="4" skill="Instructing AI"
  evidence="Team project instructions and templates"
  evidence-link="https://example.org/instructions"
  person="Example Person" as-of="2026-09-30"></stm-badge>

From a YAML profile

Export your profile as YAML from your logbook, publish it beside your page, and point the badge at it with the skill's code. The badge shows the level your evidence supports and the evidence cited. 

<stm-badge src="profile.yaml" code="AISD"></stm-badge>
